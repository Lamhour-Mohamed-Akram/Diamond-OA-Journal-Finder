What "Diamond open access" means

Diamond (or Platinum) open access journals are free for readers and free for authors: no article processing charge, no submission fee, no page charges. They are typically funded by universities, research institutes, learned societies or public programmes. Publishing in them costs nothing and keeps your work openly available, which is why funders increasingly recommend them. The quality signal to look at is the same as for any journal: peer review process, indexing (Scopus, Web of Science) and the SCImago quartile, all of which the finder shows for every title.
